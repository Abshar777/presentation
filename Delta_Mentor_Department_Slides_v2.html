FAIRNESS & PROTECTIONDELTA INTERNATIONAL TRADING ACADEMY

The 7-day meeting protection rule

Avoid competing approaches without granting permanent control over students.

No previous qualifying meeting→Eligible for CSE verification
Meeting within the last 7 days→Competing request blocked
7 days have passed→New request can be reviewed
Active payment or follow-up→Manager reviews before reassignment
Student no-show or cancellation→No completed-meeting attribution
Student requests another mentor→Documented exception possible
Key point

Day 8 does not automatically transfer an active opportunity.

In simple words

After a mentor meets a student, other requests for that student are blocked for 7 days. After that a new request can be reviewed, but if payment or follow-up is still active, a manager reviews it before anything is reassigned.
